OpenAI vs Anthropic vs xAI in 2026

By Amney Mounir

Three frontier labs dominate the same talent market, and the signals disagree. Pay favours xAI. Employees rate Anthropic highest on work-life and career growth. Candidates have the best odds at xAI and the roughest time at Anthropic.

Dataford put every available signal side by side in OpenAI vs Anthropic vs xAI: What Is the Best Company to Join?, using 800k+ interview reports plus employee ratings.

Pay, odds and experience

  • xAI posts about $645k median tech pay, against Anthropic about $555k and OpenAI about $468k.
  • Offer rates: xAI 23%, OpenAI 9%, Anthropic 4%.
  • Positive experience: xAI 51%, Anthropic 36%, OpenAI 34%. Anthropic also has the highest negative share at 38%.

Difficulty is a wash

Overall difficulty sits near 3.2 for all three: OpenAI 3.23, Anthropic 3.28, xAI 3.22. The hardest single loops in the set are Anthropic ML Engineer at 4.00 and OpenAI ML Engineer at 3.89. Getting in is not about a different difficulty score. It is about conversion and how the process feels.

Workplace ratings still matter

On employee ratings, Anthropic leads work-life (3.8), career growth (4.7) and overall (4.5). OpenAI is next. xAI sits lowest on all three. Anthropic's employee sample is only 44 ratings, so treat it as indicative. Interview samples are larger: about 830 at OpenAI, 550 at Anthropic and 130 at xAI.

How I would decide

  1. If pay is the hard constraint, xAI leads by a wide margin.
  2. If workplace ratings matter more, Anthropic is the current leader on the thin sample.
  3. Treat Anthropic and OpenAI offer rates as long shots and run parallel processes.
  4. Watch ghosting: xAI is at 7%, against 3% at the other two.

Dataford's machine learning engineer hub is a practical place to dig into role-level loops before you apply.

There is no single winner across pay, culture and odds. Pick the signal you actually care about, then prepare for a hard loop either way.

Role-level pay still moves inside each lab. xAI pays about $645k for Software Engineer, ML Engineer and AI Engineer alike. OpenAI pays about $529k for SWE and about $431k for MLE. That spread matters if you are comparing the same title across labs.

Complaint share is roughly level at 8% to 9% across the three. The sharper difference is ghosting and conversion. A 4% offer rate with a 38% negative experience share is a different product from a 23% offer rate with 51% positive reports.

I would not build a whole search around any single lab. Use the scorecard to pick which constraint you are solving for, then keep at least one higher-conversion employer on the calendar while you wait.
